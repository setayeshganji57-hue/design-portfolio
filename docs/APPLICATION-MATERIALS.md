- **Judgement:** in your thesis (chapter 4), you tested several AI logo tools, documented what each produced, and then moved to a language model for guidance, with the design work built from its suggestions. The chapter records the limits you found.
- **Process:** AI-assisted development is credited on Hamloqmeh and TradeMemory, with the concept, visual identity and interface design credited to you.

**Sentence for an interview:**

## 14. Interview talking points and likely questions

**Three stories to prepare (STAR format):**
1. **Feedback you acted on.** Which project changed direction after review, and what you did. [Use a real example from your studies or work.]
2. **A tight deadline.** Which project had the least time, and how you decided what to cut. [Use a real example.]
3. **Something that didn't work.** The thesis experiments with AI logo tools are a good example: the tools didn't produce the result you judged acceptable, so you changed the process.

**Likely questions and answer outlines:**
- *Walk me through Hamloqmeh.* Context, your role, one decision, the trial status, and what you'd do next.
- *What did you own on myMAXXI?* [Answer with your confirmed role.]
- *Why did you choose the right-to-left layout approach?* Because the audience reads in Persian. Explain how you tested the layout on phone and desktop.
- *How do you use AI in your process?* Use the sentence in section 6.
- *Tell me about your thesis.* The question of how startups communicate, the visual communication process, and what you learned about AI tools.
- *Do you have commercial experience?* [Answer from your employment history. If none, say what you have shipped or completed and what you'd bring.]
- *What are you looking for?* [Your target role, team size and kind of work.]
- *What is your salary expectation?* Give a range for the RAL that you have researched for the role and city. Ask for their range first if you can.
