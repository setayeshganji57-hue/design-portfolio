Internships are an optional route for you, not the default.

**Outreach approach:**
1. Tailored applications through the employer's own careers page, or a job board when no page exists.
2. A short personal message to the design lead for studios on your Tier A list.
3. Introductions through former classmates, your Sapienza network and former colleagues.

**Objections to prepare for:**
- *"Do you have commercial experience?"* Describe what you owned, using the facts in the portfolio. Don't inflate. Fill in the employment history before you answer.

- *"Why is the thesis title different?"* Settle the title first (see audit A5).
- *"No brand case study?"* Publish one before applying to brand roles.

## 6. How to adjust

**If applications and messages get no replies after about 10 of each:**
1. Check that the portfolio is public and works on a phone. Ask one person to open it on their own device.
2. Check role fit. If most roles ask for more than your evidence supports, move to the secondary track or a more junior title.
3. Make messages shorter and more specific. Name one detail of their work.
4. Change one variable at a time: channel, track or wording.

**If replies arrive but no interviews follow:**
1. Look at the first screen: headline, hero and first case study.
2. Make the case-study introduction shorter. Put the role and the result in the first two sentences.
3. Ask a peer for 60 seconds of feedback on the first screen.

**If interviews happen but no offers follow:**
1. Review your notes. Which question did you struggle with?
2. Rehearse the five-minute walkthrough aloud. Time it.
3. Check your salary range against the RAL for the role and city, and decide your minimum before the next interview.
4. Ask for feedback in writing after a rejection. Keep it short and polite.
5. If you are open to a stage, an apprenticeship or a VAT-number arrangement, say so, and check the terms first.

## 7. Rules that apply throughout

- Confirm your right to work before you apply to any role that requires it. This plan doesn't assume your permit covers it.
- Apply manually. Don't use automated tools on LinkedIn or any other platform. Automation breaks platform terms, and it would send applications in your name without your review.
- Don't share passwords or secrets in messages or in the repository.
- Don't send unpaid speculative redesigns. A single observation or one slide is enough.
- Do not publish the site or change its protection without your explicit decision.
